Review the pasted copy against Gioia's brand voice guidelines.

Check for:
- **Hype or buzzwords** — exaggerated claims, trendy jargon, empty superlatives
- **Vagueness** — fuzzy timelines, unclear responsibilities, hand-wavy descriptions
- **Cold or rigid tone** — overly formal, distant, or bureaucratic language
- **Missing point of view** — copy that describes without interpreting or guiding
- **Underselling the stakes** — treating branded goods as transactional rather than brand-building
- **Previous company name** — flag immediately if present

Return:
1. Overall assessment (on-brand / needs work / off-brand)
2. Specific flagged phrases with brief explanation
3. Suggested rewrites for anything flagged
