---

Draft a rebrand announcement communication for Gioia.

Ask for (or infer from context):
1. **Audience** — clients, vendors, partners, employees, or public
2. **Channel** — email, letter, social post, press release, website banner
3. **Tone** — celebratory, matter-of-fact, or somewhere in between
4. **Key details to include** — go-live date, new website, any changes to contacts or processes

Guidelines:
- Lead with what's new and exciting, not what's changing
- The previous name may be referenced only to provide continuity context ("formerly known as...")
- Reinforce that the mission, team, and quality of service remain the same — only the name and identity are evolving
- Use Gioia's brand voice: clear, confident, warm, and human
- Avoid making it feel like a legal notice — this is a moment worth celebrating
