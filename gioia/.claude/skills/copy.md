Write copy for Gioia using the brand voice defined in CLAUDE.md.

Ask for (or infer from context):
1. **Content type** — website, email, social, marketing material, or educational
2. **Goal** — what this copy needs to accomplish
3. **Audience** — which segment (marketing leader, HR, agency, procurement, complex project)
4. **Constraints** — length, format, any specific terms to include or avoid

Apply the correct tone for the content type. Default to clear, confident, and approachable.

Always:
- Speak with a point of view — don't just describe, interpret
- Emphasize solutions and outcomes over features or process
- Avoid hype, buzzwords, and vague promises
- Write as if the reader is a busy marketing leader who values clarity and respects their time

Do not reference the previous company name.
