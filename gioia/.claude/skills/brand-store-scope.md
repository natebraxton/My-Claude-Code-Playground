Help scope a Managed Brand Store recommendation for a client.

Ask for (or infer from context):
1. **Who's ordering** — employees, sales reps, marketing teams, or mixed
2. **What they're ordering** — branded merch, marketing collateral, personalized materials, or all of the above
3. **Scale** — approx number of users, number of products, number of locations
4. **Special needs** — order approvals, catalog access control, SSO, personalized products (e.g. business cards)
5. **Budget sensitivity** — is monthly hosting cost a concern?

Match against the four store tiers:
- **Pop-Up** — single campaign or event, time-limited, up to 15 products
- **Employee Store** — always-on merch for employees, up to 150 products, $50/mo
- **Marketing Store** — centralized collateral hub, up to 3 users, up to 15 products, inventory management
- **Marketing Store Pro** — unlimited products/users, access control, approval workflows, $150/mo

Return:
1. Recommended tier with reasoning
2. What's included in setup and what's additional
3. Any follow-up questions needed before scoping
