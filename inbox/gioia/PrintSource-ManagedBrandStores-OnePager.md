# Managed Brand Stores

## Your brand, always on. We handle everything else.

Managing branded merchandise and marketing materials shouldn't slow your team down. A PrintSource Managed Brand Store gives your people — employees, sales reps, or marketing teams — a single, always-available destination to order exactly what they need, on-brand and on-demand.

We scope it. We build it. We manage it. You stay focused on your brand.

---

## Find the right fit.

Every store is different, and so is every client. We'll help you identify the right solution based on how your team works, who's ordering, and what they need to order. Here's what's available.

---

**Pop-Up Store**
A focused storefront built around a single moment — a campaign launch, seasonal promotion, event, or fundraiser. It's open for a defined window of time, then closes. Clean, simple, and purpose-built.

**Employee Store**
Your branded merchandise, available to your team whenever they need it. Employees browse and order on their own terms — whether they're spending their own money, an employer-funded balance, or a mix of both. You set the catalog; they do the shopping.

**Marketing Store**
A centralized home for your marketing and sales materials — collateral, print, branded products — built for the teams who manage and distribute them. The right piece, with the right specs, every time. No more one-off requests or inconsistent files. Need personalized materials like business cards or name badges? We can build that in.

**Marketing Store Pro**
Built for organizations with a larger, more complex catalog. Full product categorization, unlimited products, and powerful tools for managing who can order what — including user groups, catalog access control, and order approval workflows. If your operation has outgrown a basic store, this is where you belong.

## A note on personalized products.

Some of the most effective branded materials include information unique to each recipient — a rep's name, office location, or contact details. We call these personalized products. Setting them up requires one-time programming to build the customization logic behind each item. This is scoped and priced per product based on complexity — it is not a recurring monthly cost. If personalized products are part of your vision, we'll walk you through exactly what's involved before anything is built.

---

## How it works.

Every store starts with a scoped build. We align on your product catalog, gather your brand assets, and configure the experience before launch. Setup fees reflect that work — the number of products, whether design is needed, and any personalized product programming. We scope everything upfront so there are no surprises. You approve before we build.
